Legal bases (GDPR)

Local storage of your notebook (trees, photos, voice notes, parking, settings) is based on performing the service you request (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)). Access to location, camera, microphone and notifications is based on your consent, given through system permissions and withdrawable in device settings (art. 6(1)(a)). Calls to map, weather, cadastre, protected-area and geocoding services are based on legitimate interest in providing the field service, and only if the corresponding API is enabled (art. 6(1)(f)). The Google Play purchase token is processed to perform the Yamadori Pro purchase contract (art. 6(1)(b)).

Retention periods

Notebook data (trees, photos, voice notes, parking, settings, local purchase token) is kept on the device until you delete it, uninstall the app or clear the app storage. Caches (map, weather, cadastre, geocoding, protected areas) are kept until you clear them in Settings, or until the cache expires. Yamadori keeps no copy on a publisher server: uninstalling removes local data.

Yamadori Manifesto

Yamadori does not sync your scouting entries to the cloud. Your field notebook lives in your pocket.

Yamadori is a field-scouting aid only. It is not legal advice, a permit, legal support, or a guarantee of accuracy. The publisher disclaims liability on that basis: you alone are responsible for checking the rules with the competent authorities.

On your device

Your notebook, your phone

No account, no Yamadori server. Your scouting entries, photos, voice notes and starting point stay on this device. On a new Android install, local encryption is on by default (KeyStore, no PIN); existing installs are left unchanged. You can turn it off in Settings — it protects trees, parking, the export password and GPS settings, but not weather or map caches.

Built for the forest

Map, weather and cadastre data are cached locally. GPS works without a network — even deep in the woods.

Protect your notebook

Export a backup regularly (USB drive, personal cloud). Without an export password, the file guarantees integrity only (checksums), not confidentiality: anyone who has the file can extract the data. With a password, the entire archive is encrypted (PBKDF2 + AES-GCM). The hint is a memory aid only; it cannot recover the password.

Full transparency

Like a paper notebook in your bag, your scouting entries remain readable on an unlocked device. Yamadori does not use Android’s automatic backup to Google. We recommend a screen lock and password-protected exports.

Android permissions

Camera: tree photos only, when you tap Capture. Microphone: voice notes (30 s max), on your action. Location: scouting, map and weather, only while the app is in the foreground. Compass: heading display during capture, processed locally. The permissions wizard on first launch guides you — nothing is requested without reason.

Online

Minimal connectivity

When online, only your GPS coordinates are sent to public services for weather, agronomy, maps, cadastre, protected areas and geocoding — never your photos or notes. By country: Open-Meteo, IGN / CNIG / basemap.de / OS / swisstopo / basemap.at / OpenTopo (BE, SE, JP, AU, DK, FI, IE, CA, NZ) / PDOK BRT (NL) / Kartverket (NO) / USGS (US), EEA, Natural England, FOEN, CadGIS, Nominatim (OpenStreetMap), and others.

Third-party services consulted

Open-Meteo (forecasts and climate history), national maps (IGN FR, CNIG ES, basemap.de, Ordnance Survey / OSM fallback, swisstopo CH, basemap.at AT, OpenTopo BE/SE/JP/AU/DK/FI/IE/CA/NZ, PDOK BRT NL, Kartverket NO, USGS US), cadastre (IGN Apicarto, Catastro ES, Catasto IT, partial ALKIS DE, HMLR GB, AV/EGRID CH + ZH AVWFS, AT municipality, CadGIS BE, BRK NL, kommun SE, matrikkel NO, DGT PT, Nominatim DK/FI/IE/JP, CAPAD AU, PAD-US, CPCAD CA, DOC NZ), protected areas (IGN FR, EEA, Natural England, FOEN/Pro Natura CH, CAPAD AU, MOE/KSJ A10 JP, PAD-US, CPCAD CA, DOC NZ), Nominatim/OpenStreetMap (place labels), municipal directories by country. Yamadori has no intermediary server: your coordinates go directly to these public services, each with its own privacy policy. Your photos and notes never leave the device this way.

No tracking

No Yamadori account, no ads, no analytics (Firebase, crash reporters, etc.). No data resale. No third-party cookies: the app runs locally in an Android WebView.

You are in control

You decide who sees your spots

Sending a tree file (photos and GPS) or exporting a backup is entirely your choice. Nothing is sent to third parties automatically.

Transparent GPS

In the foreground, GPS may start at launch to prepare a location fix, then is used to scout a tree, display the map and contextualise weather. Tracking stops when the app is in the background. No location is sent to a Yamadori server.

You cut the network

Each network connection can be disabled individually in Settings. If you turn off an API, no more live requests; already downloaded cache remains available offline.

Data inventory

What Yamadori handles for your field notebook — where it is stored, what goes online and how to delete it.

GPS coordinates

Stored on device (trees, parking, position). Sent online: latitude/longitude only, to enabled APIs. Shareable via export or tree sharing. Deletion: delete the tree or clear caches in Settings.

Photos

Stored on device. Never sent online by Yamadori. Shareable via export or tree sharing. Deletion: delete the tree.

Voice notes

Stored on device. Never sent online. Shareable via backup export only. Deletion: delete the tree.

Weather and climate

Cached locally after a request. Coordinates sent to Open-Meteo when that API is enabled. Not shared. Deletion: Settings → clear weather cache.

Map tiles

Cached locally. Tile requests for the visible map area by country (IGN, CNIG, basemap.de, OS…). Not shared. Deletion: Settings → clear map cache.

Cadastre and protected areas

Cached locally. Coordinates sent to national cadastre / protected-area services when enabled. Not shared. Deletion: Settings → clear cadastre cache.

Pro purchase token

Kept locally to unlock Pro. Yamadori does not receive your payment details (Google Play). Local deletion: uninstall the app. The purchase remains tied to your Google account and can be restored.

IP address

Yamadori does not collect your IP address. It is received by the third-party services you query (maps, weather, cadastre, Google Play, GitHub Pages) as with any HTTPS request. The publisher does not store it.
